1. About Kazi

Kazi is a job board for design roles open to designers in Kenya and across Africa. It works two ways: we aggregate publicly listed roles from company career pages and job boards, and employers can submit roles directly for review. Kazi is not a recruitment agency, staffing firm, or employer of record, and never hosts job applications — every "Apply" link sends you straight to the employer's own site.

2. Using the board

Kazi is free to browse. We do our best to keep listings current and to classify each one's eligibility (who can genuinely apply) accurately, but we can't guarantee that every aggregated listing is accurate, still open, or correctly classified. Always confirm the details on the employer's own site before applying.

3. Eligibility badges

The "Open to Kenya," "Open to Africa," "Worldwide remote," and "Check restrictions" badges are a best-effort classification based on the listing's stated location and text, or on what an employer told us directly. They are not a legal or contractual guarantee that you're eligible to work in any given location — visa, work-permit, and immigration questions are between you and the employer.

4. Posting a job (for employers)

By submitting a role through "Post a job," you confirm that the role and company are real, the application link goes straight to it, and the eligibility you select genuinely reflects who can apply. We review every submission before it appears on the board and may reject or remove a listing at our discretion, including after it's published, without notice. We don't guarantee placement, how long a listing stays visible, or applicant volume.
