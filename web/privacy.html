Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026

1. What we collect

If you're just browsing the board, we don't collect personal data or run any analytics or tracking scripts. Our hosting provider may log standard technical request data (like IP address) as part of normal server operation, the same as any website.

If you submit a role through "Post a job," we collect the job details you provide and your email address. Your email is used only to review the submission and to contact you about it if needed — it's never shown publicly on the board.

2. How we use it

Solely to review and, if approved, publish employer submissions, and to reach out about a specific submission. We don't use it for marketing, and we don't sell or share it with third parties beyond what's needed to run the site (our hosting provider).

3. Where it's stored

Submissions are stored in a database on our hosting infrastructure (Fly.io). We keep it only as long as needed to review and manage the listing.

4. Cookies and local storage

See our Cookie Policy for the full picture — in short, Kazi doesn't use tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage only for your light/dark theme preference, and, for site admins, to keep the review queue signed in.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete data you've submitted at any time: hello@odana.design

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves.
