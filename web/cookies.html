Cookie Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026

1. Short version

Kazi doesn't use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics of any kind.

2. What we do use

Your browser's local storage (not a cookie, but similar in spirit — data kept in your browser rather than sent with every request) for two things:

  • Remembering your light/dark theme choice, on every page.
  • Keeping admin reviewers signed in to the review queue (the admin page only — this doesn't affect regular visitors browsing or applying to jobs).

3. Third parties

Kazi loads one third-party script — the GSAP animation library, from a CDN — used only to animate the page. It doesn't track you or set cookies.

4. Managing this

You can clear local storage at any time from your browser's settings; this just resets your theme choice back to your system default.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves.
